My Appointment On Time relies on open data and software. Thanks to their authors.

## Holiday data

Holidays for about 200 countries come from the [date-holidays](https://github.com/commenthol/date-holidays) project by commenthol and contributors. The data is published under the [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) license and the code under the ISC license. We use it transformed into our own format.

## Typeface and icons

- [Inter](https://rsms.me/inter/) typeface by Rasmus Andersson, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- Icons by [Lucide](https://lucide.dev), under the ISC license.
