This notice explains what personal data My Appointment On Time processes, why we use it and how you can exercise your rights.

## Who processes your data

The operator of My Appointment On Time is responsible for business account data. When you book an appointment with a business, that business is responsible for its schedule data and we process it on its behalf to provide the service.

## What data we process

- **Account:** name, email, encrypted password (never stored in readable form), language, time zone and, if you sign in with Google, that account's identifier.
- **Appointments:** service, date and time, name, email, phone number if you provide it, and any notes you write.
- **Notifications:** the channels you turn on (for example, Slack or Telegram) and your preferences.
- **Connected calendars:** the permissions you authorize in Google, Microsoft or Apple, stored encrypted.
- **Technical use:** IP address and access logs for security for a limited time. Site analytics use no cookies and don't identify people.

## Why we use it

- To provide the scheduling and booking service requested by the business or by you.
- To send necessary emails: verification, codes, confirmations, reminders and account notices.
- To protect the service against abuse and meet legal obligations.

We don't sell your data or use it for advertising. Product news is only sent if you ask for it, and you can unsubscribe from any message.

## Who we share it with

Only with providers we need to operate (hosting, email delivery and, if you turn them on, the calendar or messaging services you connect) and with the business you book with. Each client sees only their own appointments.

## How long we keep it

While your account is active. If you delete it, we erase your data within 30 days, except what the law requires us to keep.

## Your rights

You can access, correct and export your data or delete your account from your dashboard. For any other request, write to us from the contact page.
